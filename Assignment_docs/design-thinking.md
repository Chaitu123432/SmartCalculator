# Design Thinking

## Empathize

1. What information do you expect before applying for a loan?
2. What makes you stop or abandon a loan application?
3. Would you like suggestions to improve your eligibility if you are rejected?

## Define

How might we help customers quickly understand their loan eligibility with clear explanations before they submit a loan application?

## Ideate

### Approach 1

Use rule-based eligibility checks using income, existing loans, and requested loan amount.

### Approach 2

Integrate a credit scoring service that provides personalized eligibility and recommendations based on financial history.
