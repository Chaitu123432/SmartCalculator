# Requirements

## Functional Requirements

1. The system shall allow users to enter monthly income.
2. The system shall accept the existing loan amount.
3. The system shall accept the requested loan amount.
4. The system shall determine loan eligibility.
5. The system shall display the eligibility decision with reasons.

## Non-functional Requirements

1. Response time shall be under 2 seconds.
2. Customer financial information shall be securely encrypted during transmission.
3. The feature shall be available 99.9% of the time.

## Out of Scope

1. The feature will not approve loans automatically.
2. The feature will not perform credit score calculations internally.
